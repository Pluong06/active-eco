Troubleshooting

"License key not found" / "doesn't look like a license key"

Copy the whole key, starting with txn_, with no spaces before or after.

"Activation limit reached"

The key is already active on 3 browsers. Deactivate it in one of them, or email us and we'll reset it.

"Your subscription is canceled / past due"

Your subscription ended or a renewal payment failed. Use the manage subscription link in your Paddle receipt email to update your payment method, or buy a new plan, then click Activate again.

I lost my license key

Email from the email address you used at checkout. We'll send your key back, usually within 24 hours.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Click Manage subscription in the extension's ⭐ Pro tab, or use the link in your Paddle receipt email. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
