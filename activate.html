Troubleshooting

"Invalid license key"

Copy the whole key with no spaces before or after. Make sure you're using the key from this product's receipt.

"Activation limit reached"

The key is already active on 3 browsers. Deactivate it in one of them, or email us and we'll reset it.

"This license has expired"

Your subscription ended or a renewal payment failed. Update your payment method in your orders, then click Activate again.

I can't find my license key

Check your spam folder, or open your Lemon Squeezy orders with the email you used at checkout. Still nothing? Email with your order number.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Manage your subscription anytime in your Lemon Squeezy orders. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
